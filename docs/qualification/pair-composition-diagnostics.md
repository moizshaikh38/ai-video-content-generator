# Developmental pair composition diagnostics

Off-line diagnostic helper only. No Auto/default/quality-score integration. It reports head center range, headroom, inferred shoulder-intent extent and partial other observed face/person intersections. These are heuristic warnings, not semantic importance or verified anatomy. Source-edge shoulder limits are distinguished from cropping introduced by the candidate.

Four unit tests pass. Current six-analysis audit flags natural two-person top view for off-centering, source-edge shoulder room limitation and a fragment of the other face-associated body. Rank1 pair focus also flags another body fragment. Older Beast short pair has no such measured flags, but no-warning does NOT prove aesthetic quality or narrative relevance. Existing extra background people are not forced into a union; these diagnostics are about the proposed panel's visible composition.

No per-frame timestamp tuning, new media/model, spend or release. Choosing whether to reject/adjust/fallback needs independent temporal pixels and further tests. Do not activate from these counts alone.
