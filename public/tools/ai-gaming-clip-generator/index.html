04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
hit start.

Are these automatic VireoClip highlights?+

No. The videos on this page are manually selected and locally rendered layout and caption demos. They do not demonstrate automatic game-event detection or prove better results than another tool. The action and racing videos use authored editorial hooks, not a transcript. The commentary demo uses cleaned speech captions.

Can I use my own gameplay?+

Open VireoClip to upload a recording you own or have permission to use. Review the current upload limits and credit estimate in the app before generating.

Will every game fit a vertical crop?+

No single crop suits every game. Check the action, facecam and important HUD elements in the editor before export. Wide strategy games may need a different composition from shooters or racing games.

What do the layout buttons do?+

The layout buttons switch between two pre-rendered commentary examples. The gallery filters show different demo clips. Neither applies a preset to your account, uploads anything or starts a paid generation.

Who made the demo footage?+

The commentary recording is by Fratm. The action recording is by Premitre; the racing benchmark is credited to the SuperTuxKart authors and uploaded by PantheraLeo1359531. Source links and media licenses are below. None of these creators or game teams endorses VireoClip.
